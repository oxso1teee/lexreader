The Last Lighthouse
Глава 3 · осталось 6 мин

The storm had passed by morning, but the sea was still restless. Mara climbed the spiral stairs one last time and stood by the great lamp.

She was reluctant to leave the lighthouse. For eleven years it had been her home, her work and her refuge. Below, the boat was already waiting.
