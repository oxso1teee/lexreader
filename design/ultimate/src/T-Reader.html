Chapter 3

The Storm

The storm had passed by morning, but the sea was still restless. Mara climbed the spiral stairs one last time and stood by the great lamp. Its glass was cold, and the brass handles had turned green with salt.

She was reluctant to leave the lighthouse. For eleven years it had been her home, her work and her refuge. Below, the boat was already waiting, rocking gently against the shore.

Every night she had lit the beacon, and every night the ships had passed safely through the rocks. Now a machine would do it instead. “It never gets tired,” the man from the harbour had said.

Mara touched the lamp once more. Outside, the last of the gale pulled at the gulls, and the water flashed silver under the clouds.
